About

I'm an electrical and computer engineering student at Boston University. Most of my work sits where machine learning meets real systems: models trained on messy data, and the infrastructure that keeps them useful.

Recently that has meant training deep networks to read X-rays, building LLM agents that correlate logs during an incident, and teaching a reinforcement-learning agent to play Pac-Man. On the side, I build statistical models for sports.

I'm looking for internships and roles in machine learning engineering and applied AI.
